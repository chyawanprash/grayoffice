Build an agent that automates a real workflow handled by a company's accounting, finance, or treasury team.

Examples include:

- Closing the books
- Reconciling accounts
- Processing invoices
- Updating forecasts
- Preparing cash reports
- Gathering audit support

The goal is to take over a repetitive internal finance process end to end, including exceptions and human review.

This track focuses on internal finance operations, not consumer banking, trading, lending, or payment products.
